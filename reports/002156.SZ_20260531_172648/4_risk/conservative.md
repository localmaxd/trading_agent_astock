If the inflection fails, capital impairment is not truncated; it compounds through interest expenses and potential asset write-downs. The 15% position size ignores this compounding tail risk.

Financing balance surged 34% in 10 days to 627M RMB [Game Theory Report, IV], creating a crowded long position. When institutional supply meets leveraged retail demand, volatility amplifies rather than stabilizes.
